affiliate disclosure

last updated · august 28, 2026

glossed participates (or has applied to participate) in affiliate advertising programs, including programs operated through networks such as Rakuten Advertising and impact.com with retail partners such as Sephora and Ulta Beauty.

this means that some links to products on this site or in the glossed app are affiliate links: if you click one and make a purchase, glossed may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you. this disclosure is made in accordance with the FTC's endorsement guides (16 CFR part 255).

what commissions never touch

glossed's whole point is honest ordering. so, stated plainly:

rankings, shade-fit evidence, and community counts are never influenced by affiliate relationships. your shelf order comes from your own face-offs. evidence lines come from real logged data and always show their n. no product is placed, boosted, or badged because a retailer pays a commission — the commission exists so glossed can link you to the exact shade you're looking at, and that is all it buys.
