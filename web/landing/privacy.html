privacy policy

last updated · august 28, 2026

this website

this site is an informational page about glossed, an iPhone app in development. the site itself:

  • sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking scripts,
  • collects no personal information — there are no forms, sign-ups, or accounts here,
  • is served by Vercel, whose infrastructure may keep standard, short-lived server logs (IP address, user agent) to operate the service.

the app (in development)

when glossed ships, it will have its own in-app privacy policy covering exactly what the app stores and why. the principles it is being built on, stated now:

  • your journal is yours. what you log — products, shades, notes, rankings — is private to your account by default.
  • aggregates are anonymous and thresholded. community claims (like "n people wear this shade") are computed without user identifiers and only shown above a minimum count — small groups are never exposed.
  • sensitive data stays out of analytics. skin details, notes, and photos never appear in analytics events, logs, or crash reports.
  • no data sales. glossed does not sell personal data.

affiliate links

this site or the app may include retailer links through affiliate programs. clicking one may set a cookie on the retailer's site under that retailer's own privacy policy. see the affiliate disclosure.
